Copenhagen Process Science Workshop

29 January 2027/Technical University of Denmark/Kgs. Lyngby

Process Science is the interdisciplinary study of how socio-technical processes work and change. Process Science makes use of process mining, business process management, and, more generally, computer science, information systems, management, organization science, engineering, and cognitive science.

CoPS Workshop is a community-building initiative that brings together researchers and groups working on Process Science in the greater Copenhagen area.

The goal of CoPS Workshop is to create opportunities to learn about ongoing research, exchange ideas, identify common challenges, and establish collaborations across research groups as well as industry. Participants from academia, industry, and other institutions are welcome, regardless of seniority.

CoPS Workshop aims to provide an informal and welcoming forum for sharing established results and early-stage ideas, with the goal of strengthening the local Process Science community.

About

CoPS Workshop 2027 will be the first edition of CoPS Workshop and will take place at DTU on the 29th of January 2027.

The event is planned as a full day of presentations and discussions. In addition to sharing results and ongoing work, participants will have the opportunity to discuss common research interests and explore potential joint activities (academia-academia, academia-industry, and industry-industry), such as preparing project proposals, solving new industrial problems, conducting research, and participating in scientific events.

Discussion and interactive engagement will be integrated throughout the program to encourage active participation from all attendees.

Reasons to join

We are organizing a CoPS Workshop for researchers at all career stages, from early-career to senior researchers, as well as industry professionals.

  • Young researchers: Receive constructive feedback on your work, expand your professional network, and explore opportunities for future collaborations.
  • Senior researchers: Connect with fellow researchers and industry professionals, exchange ideas, and establish new research projects and collaborations.
  • Industry professionals: Network with members of the local research community, gain insights into the latest research developments, and share industry challenges to explore potential solutions together.

Participation is free, but registration is necessary. More details about the location will be announced soon.

Call for talks

Talk proposal deadline 18 January 2027
Notification 22 January 2027

We invite the submission of abstracts for about 15/20-minute talks (exact time to be decided once all submissions are in) on all topics related to process science, including but not limited to:

  • Recent research results
  • Work in progress and preliminary findings
  • Newly funded research projects
  • Research proposals seeking collaborators or partners
  • Open problems, emerging challenges, and promising research directions
  • Lessons learned from process science projects in practice
  • Unmet needs of real-world process analysis and improvement

The goal is to foster discussion and collaboration rather than to present only completed research, so contributions at all stages of development are welcome, including previously published works.

Talk proposals should consist of a title and a one-paragraph abstract. No need to submit a PDF.
